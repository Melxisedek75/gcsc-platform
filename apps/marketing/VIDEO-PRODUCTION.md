# GCSC October video package

Status: creative draft, 2026-09-28. No video has been produced or published.
The October site may use the static hero photograph until both films pass
fact-check, rights review and mobile playback checks. Do not depict an untested
wallet signature, lead payment, escrow release or AI estimate as a live product.

## Message and audience

- Audience: a homeowner who needs to describe a renovation, a contractor who
  needs to evaluate an opportunity, and a grant reviewer who needs to see an
  honest working product.
- Single message: a clear project brief leads to a more useful contractor
  proposal. GCSC is building SmartContractor to support that first exchange.
- Tone: human, grounded in a real worksite, precise, optimistic without claims
  about customers, financial returns or verified outcomes that we cannot show.
- Visual language: real construction texture, natural light, legible close-ups
  of planning, minimal on-screen copy. Avoid generic blockchain animations.

## Film A: silent hero loop (10-12 seconds)

| Time | Picture | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-3s | Wide, bright renovation site; homeowner and contractor look at the unfinished room. | Show the actual problem space. |
| 3-6s | Close-up of a marked-up plan and hands pointing to a specific change. | Show the need for a clear brief. |
| 6-9s | Contractor measures or notes the scope; homeowner confirms the plan. | Show a considered response, not a transaction. |
| 9-12s | Return to a wide framing with similar movement and light to the opening frame. | Seamless loop under hero text. |

No embedded title, voice, or sound is required. Crop-safe subjects remain on the
right; the left stays readable for the H1 and CTA. Publish only as muted inline
video with poster `assets/renovation-hero-v1.png`; on reduced-motion or low-data
connections show the poster instead. The generated hero photograph is concept
art, not evidence of a real customer or contractor.

## Film B: explainer (target 60-75 seconds)

| Time | Picture | Voiceover / on-screen message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-8s | Owner in a half-renovated room, phone notes and photos scattered. | "A home project often starts with an idea, but not a clear brief." |
| 8-17s | Contractor reviewing incomplete details, then looking back at the site. | "Contractors need the right information before they can respond well." |
| 17-30s | Real capture of the accepted pilot: owner enters a request and edits its AI-assisted draft. If not accepted, use storyboard frames labelled concept. | "SmartContractor is designed to help turn that first description into a request you can review and change." |
| 30-43s | Real capture of a privacy-safe opportunity preview, then a confirmed access state only if test-mode payment and entitlement have passed. | "A contractor can evaluate the opportunity before deciding whether to request access." |
| 43-57s | Contractor edits a proposal; owner sees it in the account. Use real captures only after the journey passes preview E2E. | "The response is a human decision, supported by a clearer starting point." |
| 57-70s | Return to the worksite; GCSC name and a direct link to SmartContractor. | "GCSC. A clearer start for construction projects." |

For the pre-launch version, add a visible end slate: "SmartContractor pilot in
development". Remove or revise it only after the exact user journey is accepted.
Never show a fake receipt, fabricated user account, invented testimonials, or
automated financial settlement. Captured screens must contain synthetic data.

## Production and acceptance

1. Approve this script, the logo/brand treatment, and the claim ledger. Obtain
   written rights for every actor, location, piece of footage, music and voice.
2. Produce an animatic before paid generation. Test comprehension with two people
   unfamiliar with the project: ask what the product does *now* and what is future.
3. Capture product screens only from an accepted preview build. Record its commit
   SHA and environment; redact names, addresses, email, keys and transaction IDs.
4. Export landscape and portrait-safe MP4/WebM variants, poster and English VTT
   captions. Keep the hero loop muted and inline; the explainer has visible play,
   pause, volume and captions controls. No autoplay with sound.
5. Test on desktop, Android Chrome, a narrow viewport, slow connection and
   reduced-motion preference. Verify that the site still explains the offer when
   video fails or is disabled. Record dimensions, duration, file size and licenses.

Provider choice, paid credits and uploading business footage or user data to a
third party are separate owner decisions. This document is provider-neutral.
