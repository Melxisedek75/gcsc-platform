# GCSC October site: creative brief and direction studies

Status: local decision draft, 2026-09-29. No direction is founder-approved,
visually tested, licensed or cleared for public use. This brief covers the
presentation site at `xprnet.org`, with an explicit path to the separate
SmartContractor product at `gcsc.store`.

## Job to be done

In the first ten seconds, a new visitor should understand the brand name, that
SmartContractor is a construction-project service in development, and whether
the next step is relevant to a homeowner or contractor. The site should show
the practical problem and proposed workflow without presenting future Web3 or
financial functions as available. The product site, not the presentation site,
owns registration, project requests, opportunity access and proposals.

## Audiences and message hierarchy

| Visitor | Immediate question | First useful action | Proof needed before stronger wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Homeowner planning a renovation | Can I describe my job without knowing construction terminology? | Explore the owner route on SmartContractor; do not promise voice or AI until accepted. | Exact-build owner create/edit/publish and privacy checks. |
| Contractor evaluating a lead | Can I tell whether the work fits before spending time or money? | Explore a redacted opportunity preview and the contractor route. | Integrated project-specific access, purchase and proposal E2E. |
| Grant reviewer or partner | What is real now, what is the pilot, and what is later research? | See the pilot boundary, evidence and separately marked roadmap/whitepaper. | Traceable demo SHA, test report, rights and claims review. |

Positioning: **GCSC is building a clearer starting point for construction
projects; SmartContractor is the pilot workflow.** The current differentiator
is the planned continuity from an owner-controlled brief to a privacy-safe
contractor preview and considered proposal, not a claim that a blockchain
transaction or AI assistant already works. The site must keep this distinction
visible without requiring the visitor to read a whitepaper.

Tone: capable, plain-spoken, specific, respectful of both roles. Avoid token
economics in the first-screen offer; avoid hype words such as "revolutionary",
guarantees, fake customer cases, unverified quotes and invented savings. Use
"building", "pilot in development" and "planned" where appropriate. All
claims are controlled by `CLAIM-LEDGER.md` and the exact release candidate.

## Three direction studies for founder selection

Each direction is a composition and interaction prototype, not finished UI.
The final hero must leave part of the next section visible at common desktop
and mobile heights, remain legible over the actual image, and work from the
poster and HTML alone when video or JavaScript is unavailable.

### A. Field note (recommended for the October pilot)

Full-bleed, rights-cleared renovation site in daylight. The camera observes a
specific unfinished space, not a cinematic abstract background. A short brand
headline and one sentence sit over clear negative space; the primary action is
"Explore SmartContractor" and a smaller text action scrolls to the process.
Below the fold: a single full-width, editorial sequence of rough description
to reviewed brief to redacted opportunity. Type is precise and restrained;
color is charcoal and clean white with one warm signal accent. The hero loop
from film Direction A can replace the still only after rights, crop, captions
and reduced-motion checks. Strength: understandable without sound or animation.
Risk: needs unusually strong documentary-style photography to avoid stock feel.

### B. The measure

An unframed close-to-wide worksite sequence: a person measures a real opening,
then compares it with a concise, synthetic work brief. The brand headline is
literal; moving detail provides energy while the HTML CTA stays fixed and
accessible. The following section lays out what each party knows before a
proposal, with no fabricated app success screen. Palette: near-black,
paper-white, sharp green and a small warm warning/action accent. Strength:
distinct physical connection to construction. Risk: mobile crop may hide the
measurement, so the still poster must be separately composed. Film Direction B
is the candidate moving asset, not a dependency.

### C. Two perspectives

One full-bleed worksite, seen first from the homeowner's question and then
from the contractor's need for context. Two short story chapters share a
consistent grid and typography, with a role-specific text CTA after each;
the opening hero still has only one primary product CTA. Colors are neutral
ink, bright white, field green and coral, without two competing gradients or
floating cards. Strength: explains the two-sided marketplace quickly. Risk:
more copy and footage, and it could imply the full lead purchase is live;
each frame needs a pilot/planned classification.

## Quality references, not templates

These are official 2026 Webby recognitions. Award status is a reference point,
not evidence that our candidate meets the same standard. The design team must
inspect each actual site before turning an observation into a rule.

1. [Pentagram Archive, Best User Experience winner](https://winners.webbyawards.com/winners/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-user-experience): benchmark for clear navigation through substantial information. Apply the principle of easy orientation, not its identity or layout.
2. [Apple iPhone 17 Pro site, Best Use of Video or Moving Image winner](https://winners.webbyawards.com/winners/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-use-of-video-or-moving-image): benchmark for motion serving a product story. Apply the requirement that motion add meaning and have a still fallback, not its footage, transitions or code.
3. [Docusign, Best Practices winner](https://winners.webbyawards.com/winners/websites-and-mobile-sites/features-design/best-practices): benchmark for content, navigation and functionality together. Apply measurable journey clarity, not its content or styling.

No GitHub theme, source code, media, logo, copy, animation sequence or layout
from these works is licensed by this brief. Build original GCSC assets and
record source/license for every external component. The existing generated
`assets/renovation-hero-v1.png` is an illustrative placeholder, not a real
customer project or rights-cleared final photograph.

## Selection and acceptance

Founder selects one direction and one film direction (A/B) independently;
mixing is allowed only after the first-screen hierarchy is tested. Evaluate
each direction with one new homeowner, one contractor and one reviewer on:
brand recall, correct understanding of pilot status, ability to find the right
CTA within ten seconds, trust, and any falsely inferred live payment/wallet
capability. Record the actual observations, not a self-assigned score.

Before L18c/L18d completion: produce desktop and 390px/320px visual proofs,
contrast and keyboard/reduced-motion checks; verify all CTA destinations
against accepted routes, test loading without video/JavaScript, and inspect
cropping, text fit and next-section visibility. Independent design review and
rights/claim gates remain separate from local implementation.
