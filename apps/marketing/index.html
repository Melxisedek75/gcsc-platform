SmartContractor pilot in development

The starting point

Less uncertainty.
More clarity.

Homeowners know what needs to change, but describing the work can be difficult. Contractors need enough context to decide whether a project is right for them. SmartContractor is being designed around that first exchange.

We begin with the essentials: a project request, a relevant opportunity, and a proposal the homeowner can review. Broader financial infrastructure remains on the roadmap, not in the current pilot.
